The GIEP vs. IEP Priority

In Pennsylvania, if a child is 2e, the Individualized Education Program (Chapter 14) takes precedence over the GIEP (Chapter 16). However, the district is legally required to include gifted services within that IEP. Do not let your child's strengths be "traded" for their needs.
