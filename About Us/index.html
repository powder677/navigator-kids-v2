We Are Not "Experts."
We Are Parents Who Were Tired.

We're not doctors, therapists, or educational consultants. We're parents who got tired of being told our brilliant kids were "too much."

It didn't start with a business plan. It started in a second-grade classroom with a parent-teacher conference that many of you know too well.

My daughter—brilliant, high-energy, and intensely wired—was dimming. The teacher was kind, but she was overwhelmed. She saw a child who was "disruptive" and "unfocused." I saw a bored mind that was spinning its wheels looking for traction.

"She doesn't need less work," I realized in that moment. "She needs different work."

When professional advice failed ("Have you tried a sticker chart?"), we built our own solutions. We discovered that AI could amplify what we already knew about our children—turning our raw observations into professional advocacy scripts, calm-down plans, and educational hacks.

We built this platform because we needed it in our own homes. And we knew we weren't the only ones.
