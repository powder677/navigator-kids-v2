A Peek Inside the Kit

Co-Regulation Script Sample

"I am right here."

"You are safe."

"There is no rush."

"You are not in trouble."

"We will get through this."

Delivery: Speak slowly. Leave 10+ second pauses between phrases. Low pitch, quiet volume. Do not expect a response.
