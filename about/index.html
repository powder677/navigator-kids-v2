Your Child's Brain Didn't Come With a Manual

Navigator Kids AI helps parents of twice-exceptional children understand their child's unique brain wiring — and find tools that actually work for how they think.

Why We Built This

If you have a child who's clearly bright but struggles in ways that don't make sense, you know how isolating it can be. The teachers say they're "just not trying." The parenting books don't work. The advice that helps other kids backfires with yours.

Twice-exceptional (2e) children — kids who are gifted AND have learning differences, ADHD, autism, sensory processing differences, or other challenges — need a different approach. Their brains are wired differently, and they need tools that work WITH that wiring, not against it.

We believe every parent deserves practical, research-backed tools that match their child's actual brain — not generic advice that assumes all kids work the same way.

Navigator Kids AI exists to give you those tools. Through our brain profile quiz, you can identify how your child processes the world. Then we match you with strategies, scripts, and activities designed specifically for their type of mind.

Our Approach

Brain-First, Not Behavior-First

Most parenting resources focus on changing behavior. We focus on understanding the brain that's driving the behavior. When you understand WHY your child reacts the way they do, you can respond in ways that actually help — instead of accidentally making things worse.

Profiles, Not Labels

Our 6 brain profiles (Intense Feeler, Reluctant Starter, Deep Diver, Sensitive Observer, Bold Explorer, and Big Picture Thinker) describe patterns, not permanent identities. Most kids show a primary profile with secondary traits. The goal is understanding, not boxing in.

AI-Powered, Parent-Guided

We use AI tools to help you generate personalized strategies, but you're always in control. Our AI prompt packs give you ready-to-use prompts for ChatGPT or Claude — so you can get customized help for homework, meltdowns, IEP meetings, and more.

Education, Not Therapy

We're an educational resource, not a replacement for professional support. If your child needs evaluation, therapy, or medical care, we encourage you to seek qualified professionals. Our tools complement professional support — they don't replace it.
