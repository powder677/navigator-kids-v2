🦉 Profile Type

Your child

is a Deep Diver

They aren't ignoring you. They are miles underwater in a world of their own making. Pulling them out too fast is painful.

The Monotropic Mind

Your child has an "Interest-Based Nervous System." When they are engaged, they enter a state of deep flow that is almost impossible to break.

  • They don't just "like" things; they obsess over them (Dinosaurs, Minecraft, Space).
  • Small interruptions cause disproportionately big explosions.
  • They often don't hear their name when focused (auditory filtering).
  • Transitioning from a preferred task to a required task is their biggest struggle.
🚇

The Tunnel: Once they enter the tunnel of focus, the rest of the world disappears.

⚡

The Jolt: Being interrupted feels like a physical shock to their system.

📚

The Encyclopedia: They can retain massive amounts of data on topics they love.

This focus is a superpower, but it needs a pilot.

Without tools to manage transitions, they live in a constant state of friction with the schedules of the outside world.

Try This Tonight: "Build a Bridge"

Never ask a Diver to surface instantly. Give them a bridge to walk across.

1Instead of: "Dinner time, turn it off NOW!" (The Jolt)

2Say this: "I'm entering your pod... [Touch shoulder]. Pause is coming in 2 minutes. Find a save point." (The Bridge)

By entering their world before asking them to join yours, you respect their focus.
