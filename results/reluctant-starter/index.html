🐢 Profile Type

Your child

is a Reluctant Starter

They aren't lazy. They are stuck. Their brain treats a simple demand like a threat, causing them to freeze before they can even begin.

The "Lazy" Myth

Your child struggles with Task Initiation. The hardest part of any job isn't doing the work—it's the mental hurdle of starting it.

  • "I'll do it later" is their automatic safety phrase.
  • They can hyperfocus on things they love (LEGOs, gaming) but freeze on "easy" chores.
  • Multi-step directions make them shut down completely.
  • They need constant "body doubling" (you sitting there) to get work done.
🧱

The Wall: An invisible barrier that makes starting homework feel physically impossible.

❄️

The Freeze: When overwhelmed, their brain goes offline and they look "spaced out."

⚖️

The Negotiator: They will spend more energy arguing about the task than doing it.

This is not a character flaw. It's executive dysfunction.

Without tools to "grease the wheels" of initiation, they begin to internalize the label "lazy" or "bad kid."
