Try This Tonight: The "Co-Regulation Anchor"

You cannot reason with a storm. When the intensity spikes, stop talking and start anchoring.

1Instead of: "Calm down, it's not a big deal!" (Dismissive)

2Say this: "I am here. I am calm. I will wait with you until the storm passes." (Safety)

By staying calm, you lend them your nervous system until theirs can come back online.
