Parent Dashboard

What learned today

🎯 Skills Practiced

1. Interoception (Body Clues)

Recognizing physical sensations that signal emotional escalation. This is the foundation of emotional regulation - you can't manage what you don't notice.

2. Diaphragmatic Breathing (Dragon Breath)

Slow, deep breathing activates the parasympathetic nervous system, physically calming the body. The "smell the flower, blow the candle" cue works anywhere.

3. Progressive Muscle Relaxation (Lemon Squeeze)

Tensing and releasing muscles releases physical tension and signals safety to the nervous system. This is especially helpful for kids who hold tension in their bodies.

4. Environmental Regulation (Safe Spot)

Having a designated calm-down space gives children agency and a concrete action to take when overwhelmed.

💬 Conversation Starters
  • "Can you teach ME Dragon Breath?"
  • "Where should we set up your safe spot at home?"
  • "Let's do Lemon Squeeze together before bed!"
  • "What body clues do you notice when you're getting frustrated?"
🏠 Practice Ideas
  • Low-stakes practice: Do Dragon Breath at bedtime when calm
  • Role reversal: Let your child "teach" you the tools
  • Visual reminder: Print the recap card and post it in their room
  • Cue phrase: Instead of "calm down," try "Do you need your Super Tools?"
⚠️ When the Volcano Still Blows

That's okay and expected! These tools take repetition. When your child is already flooded:

  • Stay calm yourself (co-regulation)
  • Don't try to teach in the moment
  • Wait for the storm to pass
  • Reconnect after: "That was a big volcano. Want to practice our tools together?"
