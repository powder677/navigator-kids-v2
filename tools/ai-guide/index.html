What is AI and Why Should You Care?

Artificial Intelligence (like ChatGPT) isn't just for techies. For parents of 2e children, it's like having a research assistant, a behavioral consultant, and a sympathetic ear available 24/7. It can take your raw observations and turn them into structured plans.

Getting Started

Go to chatgpt.com and create a free account. You don't need the paid version to get incredible results. Once you're in, you just talk to it like a person.

Your First Prompt

Try this prompt:
My 8-year-old is twice-exceptional with ADHD and giftedness. They have a meltdown every morning getting ready for school. Can you help me create a visual routine that builds in transition warnings and sensory breaks?

Tips for Better Results

The more context you give, the better the advice. Tell the AI your child's age, their specific profile (like "Intense Feeler"), and what has or hasn't worked in the past.
