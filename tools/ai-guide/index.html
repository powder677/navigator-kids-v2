1 What is AI and Why Use It?

AI assistants like ChatGPT and Claude are like having a knowledgeable friend available 24/7. You type questions or describe situations, and they respond with helpful, personalized advice.

For 2e parents, AI is especially powerful because:

  • It's available at 2am during a meltdown
  • It never judges your parenting
  • It can be customized for YOUR specific child
  • It generates ideas you might not think of alone
  • It's infinitely patient with follow-up questions

3 Write Effective Prompts

A "prompt" is just what you type to the AI. Better prompts = better responses. Here's the formula:

The Basic Formula

Context + Situation + What You Need = Great Response

❌ Vague Prompt: "How do I help my kid with homework?"
✅ Specific Prompt: "My 8-year-old has ADHD and is a Deep Diver brain type. He hyperfocuses on Minecraft but refuses to start homework. Tonight he needs to do 15 minutes of math practice. Give me a step-by-step script for transitioning him from screen time to homework without a meltdown."

Key Elements to Include

  • Child's age - Strategies differ by developmental stage
  • Brain profile - From our quiz (Intense Feeler, Deep Diver, etc.)
  • Any diagnoses - ADHD, ASD, anxiety, etc.
  • Specific situation - What's happening right now
  • What you want - Script? Ideas? Explanation?

5 Common Questions

Is it safe to share details about my child?

Use first names only (or pseudonyms). Don't share identifying details like school names, addresses, or full names. AI doesn't "remember" your conversations by default.

What if the AI gives bad advice?

AI is a tool, not a replacement for professional guidance. Use your parental judgment. If something feels off, ask for alternatives or consult a professional.

Can I use this during a meltdown?

Yes! Our prompts are designed for real-time use. Keep them bookmarked on your phone for quick access.

How is this different from Google?

Google gives you articles to read. AI gives you personalized, conversational responses tailored to YOUR specific situation. It's like having a consultant vs. searching a library.
