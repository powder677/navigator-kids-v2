What's Inside the Battle Plan

Seven deliverables. One goal: you never leave a meeting wishing you'd said something different.

1

The Diagnostic Assessment

A deep-dive intake questionnaire built for 2e families—not a generic form. We map your child's profile across eight domains: Academic Output vs. Potential, Executive Function, Social-Emotional, Sensory, Communication, Behavioral Patterns, Home vs. School Presentation, and Current Service Gaps.

Our AI cross-references your responses against IDEA eligibility criteria, your state's specific regulations, and current 2e research to identify exactly where the school is under-serving your child.

$200 value
2

The Heat Map Report

A visual Red / Yellow / Green dashboard across all eight domains. But the colors aren't the point—the explanations are.

  • Each red flag includes: What it means in plain English, why it warrants formal testing, the specific evaluation to request (e.g., "Functional Behavior Assessment" or "Psychoeducational re-evaluation targeting Processing Speed"), and the clinical language to use with the team.
  • Each yellow flag includes: What to monitor, what data to collect at home, and the trigger point for escalation.

Delivered as a printable PDF. Bring it to the meeting.

$300 value
3

Your Personalized Meeting Script

Not generic "tips." A tailored script based on your child's specific red flags:

  • Opening statement for the meeting (sets the tone immediately).
  • "If they say X, you say Y" counter-moves for the exact pushback your district is likely to give.
  • Priority questions ranked by importance for your case.
  • State-specific legal citations you can reference by name. ("Per [Your State Code Section], the district is required to...")
$150 value
4

Four Pre-Written Emails (Ready to Send)

Pre-filled with your child's details, your state's legal requirements, and the specific language that creates a legal paper trail:

  • Evaluation Request Letter — if you need to request testing.
  • 24-Hour Follow-Up Email — the "paper trail" email sent the day after the meeting.
  • PWN Challenge Email — if the school refuses a request and the Prior Written Notice is incomplete.
  • Data Request Email — for ongoing progress monitoring ("Please send the raw data sheets for Goal X by Friday").
$100 value
5

The Live Strategy Call (30–60 Minutes)

This is where everything comes together. A one-on-one video call where we:

  • Walk through your Heat Map together—what to push hardest on, what to let go.
  • Roleplay the difficult conversations so you've heard the pushback before the meeting.
  • Adjust strategy based on your specific school, district, and team dynamics.
  • Build your confidence. (This part matters more than you think.)

The call is recorded. Re-listen the night before the meeting.

$250 value
6

State-Specific Legal Brief

A one-page cheat sheet for your state. Not "federal guidelines"—the actual statutes and deadlines your district must follow:

  • Evaluation timelines (calendar days vs. school days—it matters).
  • Recording laws (can you record the meeting? Do you need permission?).
  • Key case law relevant to 2e students in your jurisdiction.
  • Escalation pathway with contact info for your state's Department of Education complaint process.
$150 value
7

Your Parent Concerns Statement (Pre-Written)

This is the only section of the IEP you control. We draft it for you based on your child's profile—professional, specific, and legally airtight. It goes directly into the IEP document as part of the permanent record.
