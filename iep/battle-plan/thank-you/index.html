Academic Output vs. Potential

This is often where 2e kids get missed. Strong test scores or verbal ability can hide real struggles.

Why this matters: If your child's ability is in the 90th percentile but their output is at the 50th, that gap IS the adverse effect on educational performance—even if the report card says "B."

Executive Function

The invisible skills that school demands every single day—and that many 2e kids are silently drowning in.

Why this matters: Executive function deficits are the #1 hidden driver of 2e underperformance. A child can have a 140 IQ and still not be able to start a worksheet without 5 prompts.

Social-Emotional Functioning

How your child navigates feelings, friendships, and the emotional weight of school.

Why this matters: "Educational performance" under IDEA includes social-emotional functioning. A child who is anxious, isolated, or melting down daily is not accessing FAPE—regardless of their grades.

Sensory & Communication

How your child's body processes the world, and how they express what they need.

Behavioral Patterns & the Home-School Gap

This is the one that makes or breaks 2e IEP cases. If your child looks "fine" at school but falls apart at home, this section is everything.

The Masking Problem: Many 2e kids hold it together at school through sheer willpower, then explode the second they're in a safe environment. The school sees a "good student." You see a child who is depleted. This gap is critical data for your case.
