Jurisdiction: California

California: The "High-Conflict" State

In California, "good grades" are often used to deny services. We use Ed Code 56341.1 and case law like Parent v. Poway to fight back for your child.

⏱️ The 15-Day "Assessment Plan" Rule

Action CA Deadline Parent Strategy
Request Evaluation 15 Days to respond. If the district refuses to assess your child, they must provide a Prior Written Notice. They cannot just "ignore" the email.
Evaluation Period 60 Days to hold meeting. This creates a hard deadline for the eligibility meeting.

🎤 The Absolute Right to Record

The Statute: CA Ed Code 56341.1(g)(1)

The Rule: You have a statutory right to audio record the IEP meeting, BUT you must notify the district in writing at least 24 hours in advance.

The Script: "Pursuant to Ed Code 56341.1, I am providing notice of my intent to audio record the upcoming IEP meeting for my child." (Send this email 48 hours prior to be safe).

⚖️ The 2e Precedent: Parent v. Poway

In Parent v. Poway Unified School District, the court ruled that a student cannot be disqualified from Special Education solely because they have average or above-average grades.

The Takeaway: If your district says, "He/She has B's, so he/she doesn't qualify," cite this case. Educational performance includes behavior, anxiety, and social skills—not just the report card.

The GATE vs. SPED "Silo"

In districts like LAUSD, schools often force parents to choose between GATE (Gifted) and Special Ed.

The Reality: Your child is entitled to Dual Services. Giftedness does not erase disability. If they try to remove GATE services because of an IEP, they are violating "Least Restrictive Environment" (LRE) rules.
