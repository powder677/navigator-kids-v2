Evaluation Timelines in Katy ISD

Navigating FIE delays, extension requests, and high expectations in Texas's premier growth district.

High Expectations in the Energy Corridor

Katy Independent School District (KISD) is a powerhouse of suburban growth, serving nearly 95,000 students. Fueled by the "Energy Corridor" economy, the district is characterized by an affluent, highly educated community that demands excellence. From the $72 million Legacy Stadium to its top-tier academic rankings, KISD operates with a "go big" mentality.

However, this rapid growth creates significant operational strain. As enrollment surges toward 100,000, the hiring of specialized staff—Educational Diagnosticians and Licensed Specialists in School Psychology (LSSPs)—often lags behind. For parents of children needing special education, this administrative bottleneck often manifests in a specific, frustrating document: the timeline extension request.

The "FIE Extension" Dilemma

Procedural Safeguards

Parents searching for information on a Katy ISD FIE evaluation timeline extension request are typically at a crossroads. The district has likely informed you that due to high volume, they cannot complete your child's Full Individual Evaluation (FIE) within the state-mandated timeframe.

The 45-Day Rule

Under Texas law, the district generally has 45 school days from the date they receive your signed written consent to complete the FIE. They then have 30 calendar days to hold the ARD meeting.

Should You Sign the Extension?

  • It's Voluntary: You are not required to agree to an extension unless specific exceptions apply (e.g., student absences). If the delay is strictly due to district staffing shortages, you can say no.
  • Leverage for IEE: If the district misses the deadline and you have not granted an extension, you may be in a strong position to request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) at public expense.
  • Read the Fine Print: Ensure any extension has a hard, specific new deadline. Never sign an open-ended extension.

If the deadline has already passed, review our guide on what happens when Texas schools miss deadlines.

Don't Get Swept Away by the Current

Typhoon Texas, adjacent to Katy Mills Mall, is a symbol of the local lifestyle—family-oriented, active, and fun. But anyone who has visited knows that waiting in long lines is often part of the experience. While waiting for a water slide is annoying, waiting for educational intervention can be detrimental.

In a district growing as fast as Katy, it is easy for a single student's file to get swept away in the current of administrative backlog. The FIE timeline is your safety mechanism. It ensures that your child is prioritized.

Katy ISD prides itself on excellence. As a parent, holding the district to the statutory timeline is not "being difficult"—it is ensuring that the district's high standards apply to special education services just as rigorously as they apply to varsity athletics or AP courses.
