The NY laws behind these deadlines

NY Ed Law §4402(1)(a)
District must begin evaluation within 60 days of receiving written consent
8 NYCRR §200.4(b)(1)
Evaluation must be completed within 60 calendar days of consent
8 NYCRR §200.4(d)(2)(i)
CSE meeting must occur within 30 days of completed evaluation
8 NYCRR §200.4(e)(1)(ii)
IEP services must begin as soon as possible, no later than start of next quarter or 30 days
NY Ed Law §4402(1)(b)(1)
Annual review of IEP required — within 12 months of last meeting
8 NYCRR §200.4(b)(4)
Re-evaluation required at least every 3 years unless parent and district agree otherwise
