The District 06 IEP Environment

[cite_start]District 06 serves as a vibrant Dominican hub with a high English Language Learner (ELL) population[cite: 1, 2]. [cite_start]Families here face a specific systemic hurdle: bilingual special education assessments are often backlogged by months or even years[cite: 1, 2].

Parents must be proactive in asserting ELL special education rights to ensure language barriers do not mask a need for support. [cite_start]Understanding the New York IEP timeline is critical when waiting for bilingual evaluations[cite: 1, 2].
