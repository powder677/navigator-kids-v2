The District 09 IEP Reality

District 09 is a high-need area with significant charter school penetration. A critical pain point for families is the phenomenon of charter schools "counseling out" students with special needs, often resulting in sudden mid-year transitions back to traditional public schools.

When these transitions occur, maintaining your child's service level is vital. Parents often face "crisis-management" scenarios and must leverage the New York IEP timeline to ensure the DOE provides immediate placement and continuity of services.

"In District 9, many parents feel their child is pushed out of charters right before state testing, leaving the local zoned school to pick up the pieces mid-year."
