The District 07 IEP Reality

District 07 is characterized by a high density of charter schools and significant economic challenges. Families here often face a systemic failure to identify twice-exceptional (2e) students, as the local educational focus remains largely on remedial support rather than identifying giftedness alongside disability.

Because of the high charter penetration, parents frequently find themselves navigating the complex choice between Charter services and traditional DOE offerings. It is essential to understand how to request an IEP in New York to ensure your child isn't overlooked in a high-need environment.

"In District 7, if your child is smart but struggling, they often only see the struggle. We have to fight to show the whole picture."
