District 11 is anchored by local landmarks such as PS 83 Donald Hertz.

The District 11 IEP Reality

District 11 serves a stable, middle-class Bronx demographic, including the massive residential community of Co-op City. While housing is stable, families often struggle with a severe lack of specialized ASD Nest and Horizon program seats.

This shortage frequently forces families into "out-of-district" placements, increasing commute times for students requiring specialized autism support. Navigating the New York IEP timeline is essential here to secure these limited seats before they are filled by students from across the borough.

"In District 11, if your child needs a Nest program, you're often told there's no room at the local school. You have to be prepared to fight for a seat or a bus to another district."
