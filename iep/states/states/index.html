Federal Law is the Floor.
Your State Might Have a Ceiling.

IDEA sets the minimum rights you have. Your state may offer more protection, faster timelines, and stricter rules for schools. Don't leave that leverage on the table.

Why "Local" Matters in a Federal Law

Many parents assume Special Education is the same everywhere. It isn't. While the definition of a disability comes from federal law, the procedures are often state-specific.

Timelines

Federal law says evaluations must be done in 60 days. Some states (like PA) say 60 calendar days; others say 45 school days. Knowing the difference prevents the school from stalling.

Consent

In some states, you must sign for placement only. In others (like MA), you can reject specific parts of an IEP while keeping others active.

Recording

Can you record the meeting? Federal law is silent. Your state's "Wiretap Laws" decide if you need the school's permission or if you can just hit record.
