The IEP "Go-Bag" Checklist

An IEP meeting is a business negotiation. You wouldn't walk into a contract negotiation without your files. Do not walk into this meeting without yours.

Phase 1

One Week Before

AI Power Move

Drafting Your Statement with AI

Prompt: "I need to write a 'Parent Concerns' statement for my child's IEP. He/She is 2e (gifted/ADHD). His/Her strengths are math and coding. His/Her struggles are task initiation and sensory overload. Please write a professional 1-paragraph statement that highlights his/her potential but clearly states that without support for his/her executive dysfunction, he/she cannot access the curriculum."

Phase 2

During the Meeting

🎒 What's in Your Bag?

  • ✔ Your Binder (current IEP, evaluations, work samples).
  • ✔ A Photo of Your Child: Place it on the table. It reminds everyone this is about a human, not a file number.
  • ✔ A Notepad & Pen (or laptop).
  • ✔ Your list of Strategic Questions.
Phase 3

The 24-Hour Follow Up

The meeting isn't over when you leave the room. The paper trail is your safety net.
