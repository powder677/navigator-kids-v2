What does a 2e child need to thrive?

Short answer: Success requires a three-part approach: understanding their unique brain profile (Quiz), securing legal accommodations at school (IEP), and mastering co-regulation strategies to manage meltdowns at home (Regulation).

What Do I Say During a Meltdown?

Short answer: Say less. Lower your voice. Get on their level. Use the exact scripts in our free Emergency Regulation Kit—phrases designed for the moment when your child's thinking brain goes offline.
