Meet Cosmo the Space Pup

Cosmo dreams of exploring the universe! This imaginative space pup has big plans—rocket ships to build, planets to discover, and inventions to create. The only problem? Sometimes Cosmo gets so excited about the BIG idea that the little steps get a bit... fuzzy.

"I know EXACTLY what the finished rocket will look like! The building part? That's just... details."

If your child has brilliant visions but struggles with step-by-step execution, they'll love adventuring with Cosmo while learning to connect their big dreams to small actions.
