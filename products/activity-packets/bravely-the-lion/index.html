Meet Bravely the Lion

Bravely isn't afraid of anything—well, almost anything. This fearless lion loves to climb the tallest trees, explore unknown caves, and try things nobody else will try. Sometimes grown-ups get worried, but Bravely knows that adventure is the best teacher!

"I'm not being reckless—I'm being BRAVE! There's a difference... I think."

If your child charges into every situation, says "yes" before thinking, and has the bumps and bruises to prove it, they'll find a kindred spirit in Bravely.
